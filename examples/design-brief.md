# Coral Daisy Flutter Dress

Original garment concept for a baby. The child setting in the SVG input controls conceptual proportions only; it does not identify a validated size.

A relaxed A-line dress with a round front neckline, a slightly higher round back neckline, short flutter sleeves and a softly flared skirt. The coral ground carries a small cream daisy print, with warm ochre flower centres. Two cream rounded patch pockets sit symmetrically on the front. Four cream buttons open the centre-back bodice to the waist, with a discreet centre seam continuing through the skirt; no closure is shown on the front. Both views share the same silhouette, sleeves, waist seam, print, hem and palette.

Suggested material appearance: lightweight matte cotton lawn with a soft drape, cream neckline binding and cream pockets. The sleeve volume and regular dress length are visual design proposals. The matching back is an original proposed view, not inferred from a supplied reference.

Palette: coral #E9877F, warm cream #FFF2DD, small ochre floral centres #BC985E.

Requested deliverable: one self-contained, editable SVG showing front on the left and back on the right on a neutral background. Individual garment outlines, pocket shapes, neckline, floral pattern and buttons remain vector elements. The accompanying JSON retains the supported renderer inputs for changes.

This is a design concept, not a validated cutting pattern, size specification or sewing instruction set.

Visual refinements to the renderer output: removed its default studio header; labelled the intended wearer as a baby; added matching sleeve fold strokes; limited the back opening to a four-button bodice placket and continued a fine centre-back skirt seam. No installed skill files were changed.
